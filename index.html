💡 Strategy Tips

📚 Learn Cribbage

🎯 Scoring Basics

Fifteens (2 points each): Any combination of cards that adds to 15

Pairs (2 points each): Two cards of the same rank

Runs (1 point per card): Three or more cards in sequence

Flush (4 or 5 points): All cards same suit

Nobs (1 point): Jack in hand matches starter suit

🧠 Discard Strategy

Your Crib: Keep cards that work together, discard complementary cards

Opponent's Crib: Discard cards that don't help them (avoid 5s, complementary cards)

Balancing Act: Maximize your hand while minimizing opponent's crib potential

🏆 Advanced Tips

Magic Eleven: Cards that add to 11 can't make 15 with face cards

Starter Strategy: Remember the starter affects both hand and crib

Pegging Prep: Consider how your kept cards will play during pegging
